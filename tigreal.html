Tigreal Guide

Tigreal

Tank / Initiator

Tigreal is a powerful tank known for his crowd control and team fight initiation. He can group enemies and set up perfect combos for his team.

Skills

Passive – Fearless

Reduces damage taken after using skills.

Skill 1 – Attack Wave

Charges forward and deals damage to enemies in a line.

Skill 2 – Sacred Hammer

Pushes enemies and stuns them on impact.

Ultimate – Implosion

Pulls nearby enemies and stuns them for team follow-up.

Advantages

• Strong crowd control
• Great team fight initiator
• Tanky and durable
• Easy to set up combos

Weaknesses

• Low damage output
• Depends on team follow-up
• Can be countered by mobility
• Ultimate needs timing

Best Build

Tough Boots → Dominance Ice → Antique Cuirass → Athena’s Shield → Immortality → Oracle
