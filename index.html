Heroes Wiki

Marksman

Miya

A scaling marksman who becomes very strong in late game. She relies on positioning and timing more than early fights.

Marksman

Layla

A beginner-friendly hero with long range damage. Easy to learn, but needs protection from teammates.

Fighter

Alucard

A lifesteal-based fighter who excels in duels and picking off targets.

Tank

Tigreal

A strong initiator with crowd control skills that start team fights.

Rank System

Early Game Ranks

Warrior → Elite → Master

Focus: learning mechanics and basic map awareness.

Mid Game Ranks

Grandmaster → Epic

Focus: drafting, teamwork, and objective control.

High Ranks

Legend → Mythic

Focus: strategy, macro play, and consistency.

Meta Tier List

A Tier
