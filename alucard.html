Alucard Guide

Alucard

Fighter / Assassin

Alucard is a strong lifesteal fighter who excels in 1v1 fights. He becomes extremely dangerous when ahead in gold and levels.

Skills

Passive – Pursuit

After using skills, Alucard’s next attack deals enhanced damage and heals him.

Skill 1 – Ground Splitter

Jumps and deals AoE damage while slowing enemies.

Skill 2 – Whirling Smash

Deals spinning damage and increases lifesteal potential.

Ultimate – Fission Wave

Massively increases lifesteal and damage output for dueling.

Advantages

• Extremely high lifesteal
• Strong 1v1 duelist
• Snowballs easily
• Good sustain in fights

Weaknesses

• Weak against crowd control
• Struggles vs kiting heroes
• Needs early advantage
• Falls off if behind

Best Build

Warrior Boots → Hunter Strike → Endless Battle → Blade of Despair → Malefic Roar → Immortality
