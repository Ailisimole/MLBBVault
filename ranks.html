Rank System

Warrior

Entry rank. Learn basic mechanics and hero control.

Elite

Beginner ranked gameplay with simple matchups.

Master

Players start learning roles and map awareness.

Grandmaster

More competitive games and basic drafting awareness.

Epic

Draft pick begins. Team composition matters more.

Legend

High skill matches with stronger teamwork required.

Mythic

High-tier ranked gameplay with skilled players.

Mythic Honor

Advanced competitive level with strong coordination.

Mythic Glory

Elite-level gameplay with highly skilled players.

Mythic Immortal

Top of the leaderboard. Absolute high-tier players.
